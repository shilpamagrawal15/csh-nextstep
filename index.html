HESC Guidelines

Essay Advice & Financial Aid Appeal Tips

Expert strategies for writing financial hardship statements, appeal letters, and scholarship essays.

Expert Writer Tips
1

Be Honest & Specific About Need

Clearly describe any special financial circumstances (unreimbursed medical bills, job change, caring for family members) that tax forms alone do not show.

Tip: Focus on recent changes
2

Quantify Impact With Numbers

Instead of stating general hardship, provide clear numbers (e.g., exact net income reduction or monthly essential costs) to help financial aid officers calculate your true need.

Tip: Attach documentation
3

Stay Positive & Goal-Oriented

Highlight your dedication to your educational goals, academic strengths, and how receiving additional aid will help you succeed at their institution.

Tip: Highlight resilience
Financial Aid Appeal Letter Structure

If your FAFSA or TAP financial aid offer falls short, you can write an official **Special Circumstances Appeal Letter** to your college's financial aid office. Follow this simple 4-paragraph template:

Paragraph 1: Gratitude & Commitment

Thank the college for accepting you, express enthusiasm, and confirm it is your top choice.

Paragraph 2: Explanation of Hardship

Detail specific financial changes since prior-prior tax year with dates and facts.

Paragraph 3: Documents Attached

List supporting evidence (layoff notices, medical receipts, revised tax returns).

Paragraph 4: Respectful Request

Respectfully request a re-evaluation of your aid package and offer to answer questions.
